# Youi

> Youi is an Australian general insurance carrier headquartered at Sippy Downs on the Queensland Sunshine Coast, authorised by APRA and operating under AFSL 316511 (ABN 79 123 074 733). It writes personal and small-business property and casualty lines direct to consumers - car, NSW CTP Green Slip, SA CTP, motorcycle, caravan and trailer, watercraft, home building and contents, and small business cover including public liability. Youi is a wholly owned subsidiary of Youi Holdings Pty Ltd, part of OUTsurance International Holdings, with the South African-listed OUTsurance Group as ultimate holding company.

Important for agents: Youi publishes NO public API. There is no developer portal, no OpenAPI or Swagger document, no GraphQL endpoint, no webhook or event catalog, no public Postman collection, and no SDK in any package registry. Quote, bind, issue and FNOL are all consumer web and telephone flows only; there is no partner-facing or agent-facing programmatic surface. Do not attempt to construct API calls against Youi hosts - portalapi.youi.com.au is a private first-party backend for the customer portal SPA and is not a published interface.

Disambiguation: Youi (youi.com.au), the Australian insurer described here, is NOT You.i TV / You.i Engine (youi.tv), an unrelated Canadian software company whose developer documentation does exist. Package registries returning "youi" results almost always mean You.i TV, not this company.

- [Start a quote](https://www.youi.com.au/quote-start): Consumer web quote flow. No programmatic quote API.
- [Claiming](https://www.youi.com.au/claiming): Consumer FNOL and claims flow. No programmatic FNOL API.
- [Customer portal](https://portal.youi.com.au/): Angular single-page policy self-service app behind a login (noindex).
